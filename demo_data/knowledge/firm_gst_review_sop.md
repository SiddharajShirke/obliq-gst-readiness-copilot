# Sharma & Associates GST Review SOP

## Invoice review

Compare the original invoice with the extracted fields. Check GSTIN format, invoice number, invoice date, taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, cess, and invoice total. If the total does not equal the taxable value plus tax components within the prototype tolerance, keep the finding open until a reviewer resolves it.

## Period and duplicates

Flag invoices outside the selected GST period. Detect possible duplicates using supplier GSTIN, normalized invoice number, invoice date, and invoice total. A format check does not prove that a GSTIN is active on the official portal.

## Human approval

No extracted data should be treated as approved until a CA or reviewer verifies it. Every client reminder must remain in an awaiting-approval state until a staff member approves or edits it.
